Shipping a block editor in six weeks

By Dana Reyes · October 12, 2026

When we started, the plan was simple: wrap an existing editor, add our toolbar, and ship. Six weeks later we had our own HTML engine, a paste pipeline, and a fixture suite built from real documents our users sent us.

Why paste mattered most

Most content arrives by paste. People copy from Google Docs, Word, wikis, and web pages, and every source brings its own markup: wrapper spans, inline fonts, tracking links, and page furniture like share bars and related-post lists.

A shadow box is not an ad: the cleanup splits class names into tokens, so shadow never matches ad.

The best paste is the one you don't have to fix afterwards.

What we measured

  • Time to first edit after a paste
  • How many blocks needed manual fixes
  • The fidelity score from the import report
